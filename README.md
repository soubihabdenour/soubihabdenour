I am a **Master’s researcher in Machine Learning Security** at **Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), South Korea**, specializing in **Federated Learning, privacy leakage, and robustness under real-world constraints** 🔐🤖.

My work focuses on **federated learning under heterogeneous data**, **poisoning and membership inference attacks**, and **privacy-preserving ML mechanisms** such as **Differential Privacy** and secure auditing protocols.  
I aim to bridge **theoretical security analysis** with **practical ML system design**.

I enjoy building **reproducible research pipelines**, running **large-scale FL experiments**, and stress-testing models against **realistic adversaries**.

Feel free to connect with me — always happy to discuss **FL, ML security, or research ideas**.
